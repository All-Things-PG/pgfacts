# Dave DePottie Bio

Dave DePottie is a 40 year veteran of Software Engineering. He is now Chairman of All Things PG and IT Director. Dave has been developing websites since the Internet was invented, so Phase 2 is well within the scope of his experience as a Sr. Software Engineer. Since graduating with a BSc from Brock University in Canada in 1984, Dave has held positions as Sr. Developer, Project Leader North America, Manager of Professional Services, Systems Analysts, Systems Administrator and DBA/Database Specialist, working for large corporatoins and industry leaders. His last role was managing a team in India (6) to perform a large multi-year database migration of Scientific and Medical Publications and Journals into AWS. These 60M+ record databases included PubMed (Medline), WebOfScience (WOS), ScienceWire, JCR, USPTO Patents and Grant databases from around the world. Dave invented a new merging and matching technique to create a Publications Catalog, as a single, fast searchable Knowledge Base built from Medline and WebOfScience.
