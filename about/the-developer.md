# The Developer

### Note from the Developer (Dave DePottie)

I envision a website where users come to us to solve problems. It's not just about information, it is about solutions.  PG is much more than a skin disease, and affects all areas of your life, so there are many issues to address and solve.  Those looking for information can easily do a quick AI or Google search and find information about PG. Even with that, a user may still be lost as to what to do next.  

Our website needs to be more than just information, because users may not know the right questions to ask.  They could need help and guidance, particularly say for a Caregiver.  Accurate information is vital, but that alone doesn't solve problems. Wisdom is the application of knowledge, in the same was that a solution is the application of information.  

A user could be a new patient or new caregiver, a doctor treating PG for the first time, a pharmaceutical representative curious how to do clinical trials for PG patients.  The number of scenarios is endless, and yet there is only one website.  In order to be all things about PG, we need to support whoever might be visiting the site and why.  Our current website gaves us a a great PG Foundation presense on the web, but the content is static and limited, and its up to the user to find the solution.

With Phase 2 and an enhanced user experience, we can guide someone to the information they need and how to apply it. First we need to know who is visiting the site, so the site must allow selecting their user experience to tell us who they, are without having to register. The central goal objective or goal of Phase 2 is to create a searchable Knowledge Base of accurate, vetted, curated content on any topic, for every type of user.  The more content we collect the better.  Search results will show Menu Items connected to Content that is contained in the Knowledge Base.

From my own personal experience, I started PG way back in 2015 and quickly became disabled. My wife became my caregiver overnight and was lost as to what to do. She saw me in pain and bedridden, but couldn't help. She didn't know how to treat my wounds, but learned. She worried about me every day. She researched the internet, found tons of information about PG, but not all of it was consistent or accurate. Much of the information was out of date. She kept digging non-stop and joined the FB group for PG. She shared everything she learned with the group, and she took care of me, which was a 24/7 job. My family had to chip in to cover shifts, so that I was never left alone, even in the hospital.  What a journey for all of us, and somehow we got through it.  Not everyone has the same thirst and ability for knowledge as my wife, but all are equally motivated.  A Caregiver should be able to come to the website, read caregiver stories, watch videos on wound care, and consult the knowledge base.  I shared that thirst for knowledge and learned everything I could about PG, and we  both became very active on the FB support group trying to help others learn as well. My vision for Phase 2 is to make it a resource that helps everyone.

For me, Phase 2 is about making it easier for the next caregiver, or a new patient, or a provider that has never treated PG before. How can we help a provider? We are not doctors, but we are experienced patients. Doctors can read our stories, see our pictures, and search our knowledge base to understand the emotional journey. Our current website already has valuable, trusted information about diagnosis and treatment, but we can do so much more. We need an easy way to add content, examples, stories, pictures, videos and more. All of this content, linked to menu items, will go into the knowledge base. A user of the site will enter a search and quickly get a list of all articles in the system.  That will be the core of Phase 2, coupled with an enhanced user experience, and a database to support all the new features.   A database is a critical step to offering solutions not just information. My hope is that anyone, regardless of who you are, will benefit from www.allthingspg.org.

***Right now, all design, concepts, database, proof of concept, this documentation site and so forth is proprietary, of my own invention. DCMS is something new that I created over the course of a year, on my own time and with my own computers, servers and licensed software that I personally paid for. It's my hope that Phase 2 will be fully adopted by All Things PG, with the support of our benefactors.***

Thank you!

Dave DePottie<br>
Chairman, IT Director<br>
All Things PG<br>

### Developer Experience

Dave DePottie is a 40 year veteran of Software Engineering. He is now Chairman of All Things PG and IT Director. As IT Director, Dave has used his tech experience to creating a tecnhology platform for All Things PG, using a Microsoft nonprofit grant based on M365 Business licences, and a $2000 yearly grant toward hosting websites with a database.

Through the grant, All Things PG has a technology platform that allows for unlimited growth and potential.  We have our own custom domain, and all the applications we would need,  including Exchange, SharePoint, Teams, Users and Groups, Permissions, and much more. Our new platform can host a nonprofit website of any size on any database, using the $2000 annual grant to eliminate cost.  The user license and the website will be free.  All users have their own work email address in the form firstname@allthingspg.org, and external users accounts for staff at OHSU, allowing everyone to participate in our bi-monthly virtual Board Meetings Teams meetings hosted within the ATPG domain.  Meetings are automatically recorded, with AI generates meeting notes, and everything is securely stored on the organization's 1tb OneDrive space and in SharePoint.  Everything is integrated with users, groups, teams, calendars and emails and more.

Given the power of the grant and the new Microsoft tools, Dave started exploring the concept of adding a database to the existing website using the new platform.  Dave has been developing websites since the Internet was invented, so this was within the scope of his experience as a Sr. Software Engineer, Systems Admin, DBA, and Systems Analyst and other roles he's filled. His primary role has always been as a Developer and Database Specialilst since graduating in 1984 at Brock University in Canada.

You may review his bio at [Dave DePottie](https://www.allthingspg.org/dave-depottie), and please see our other [Board Members](https://www.allthingspg.org/our-people) as well. As a patient organization, ATPG has considerable credentials, including many years of personal PG experience, and with Dr. Ortega as our Medical Director and a world-wide PG expert. We are an organization that is rapidly growing, and built from the volunteer spirit to give back to the PG community.
