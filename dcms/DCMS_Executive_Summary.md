# DCMS - Dynamic Content and Menuing System

## Executive Summary

### 1. What this project is

All Things PG needs a website that does more than present information. It needs a platform that can grow with the organization, serve different audiences well, and keep content maintainable over time.

The Dynamic Content and Menuing System (DCMS) is the proposed Phase 2 platform. It is designed to turn the website into a durable community resource for patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals, researchers, industry partners, and supporters.

The goal is simple:

```text
right audience
    -> right navigation
    -> right content
    -> right tools
    -> right next step
```

This is not a generic site builder. It is a focused content platform built around the real needs of the PG community.

---

### 2. Why Phase 2 matters

The first website established a public presence and gave the organization a real starting point. That was important work.

Phase 2 is about making the site substantially more useful.

People coming to All Things PG are not looking for a brochure. They are looking for:

- trustworthy PG information;
- practical support and clear next steps;
- clinician and researcher resources;
- audience-specific navigation;
- a dependable clinic finder;
- contact and participation workflows;
- a platform the organization can actually maintain.

The current site gives the organization a foundation. Phase 2 gives it a system.

---

### 3. What DCMS provides

DCMS is built around a simple idea: content and navigation should live in data, not be trapped inside page markup.

That gives the organization several advantages:

- pages can be added and reorganized without redesigning the whole site;
- different audiences can see different starting points;
- the site can grow without becoming harder to manage;
- content can be curated, reviewed, and improved over time;
- the same structure can support articles, profiles, galleries, FAQs, tools, and future content types.

The platform is intentionally flexible, but not loose. It is designed to stay understandable for the people who will maintain it.

---

### 4. Why the architecture matters

All Things PG serves more than one audience. That means the site should not force everyone through the same path.

DCMS supports audience-aware experiences so the site can speak appropriately to:

- patients who need plain-language guidance;
- caregivers who need practical support;
- providers who need efficient clinical information;
- researchers and industry partners who need structured context;
- visitors who simply need to orient themselves.

That is a better experience for users, and it is a better use of the organization’s time.

---

### 5. The platform model

DCMS separates the system into clear layers:

- **menus** define what can be reached;
- **route targets** define what the menu item means;
- **content documents** define the type and purpose of the content;
- **content elements** store the actual page blocks;
- **portal pages** provide audience-specific landing experiences;
- **layout and partials** keep the presentation consistent;
- **migration tables** allow content to be prepared and rerun as needed.

This makes the site easier to curate and much easier to evolve.

---

### 6. The business case

The business value of DCMS is not technical elegance. The value is that it helps the organization do its work better.

It supports:

- clearer communication;
- better organization of content;
- more useful visitor journeys;
- future growth without rework;
- a more trustworthy and professional presentation;
- easier updates over time;
- a cleaner path toward a managed Azure deployment;
- repeatable content migration and refinement.

For the sponsor, the important point is this:

> Phase 2 turns the website into an owned, maintainable service instead of a static collection of pages.

---

### 7. What makes the portal experience important

The portal experience is one of the strongest design ideas in the system.

Instead of asking every visitor to start from the same generic page, DCMS gives each audience a recognizable entry point with its own tone, emphasis, and navigation pattern.

That means the site can feel more personal and more useful without becoming inconsistent.

---

### 8. What happens next

The work is moving in a practical order:

1. keep the POC stable and understandable;
2. refine the content model and migration process;
3. continue building the audience-specific portal experience;
4. expand content only where it adds real value;
5. keep the system simple enough to maintain long term.

The intent is not to build everything at once. It is to build the right foundation and then extend it deliberately.

---

### 9. Success looks like this

DCMS will be successful if:

- visitors find the right information faster;
- patients and caregivers feel supported, not overwhelmed;
- providers can find material they can actually use;
- the clinic finder is dependable;
- the organization owns and can rerun its migration process;
- future changes do not require a full rebuild.

---

## Summary

Phase 1 established the organization online.

Phase 2 is about making that online presence useful, sustainable, and audience-aware.

DCMS is the proposed path to get there: a content platform that is clear, maintainable, and designed around the people All Things PG serves.
