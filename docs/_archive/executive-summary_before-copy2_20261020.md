# Executive Summary

### Overview

All Things PG (ATPG) established an early online presence in September 2025 by releasing **Phase 1** of www.allthingspg.org.  This fulfilled an urgent need to establish a PG Foundation and begin our work of Raising PG Awarenewss. The website created visibilty as a nonprofit organization, gave us credility within the a rare-disease community, and provided needed informational support to those affected by Pyoderm Gangrenosum (PG). Phase 1 of the website has been well received. It nicely illustrates our brand, and offers good information about PG to patients and caregiver, and additional features like Surverys, Newletters and a Clinic Finder. 

Phase 1 was built using a custom cms tool that allows add/edit of web pages, surveys, newletters, providers, news and events, and a list of the Board of Direcotrs and their Bios.  It contains basic informaton about PG, including What is PG, Diagnosis and Treatment for patients and caregivers.  It is consider a static website, meaning it delivers prebuilt html pages without executing server side code or querying a database. All information entered using the tool is stored as lists in a **flat-file** (not structured data), and these flat-files are used by different tool to generate the static HTML pages that are deployed to the AWS hosting environment.  We do not have access to the website hosting platform or the site generation tool. That is all behind the scenes and is proprietary.

This means we are limited in scope to what the cms editing tool currently provides.  Although tedious at times, it does do a good job of entering information into the website, such as more newsletters, or more providers for the Clinic Finder (currently only 6), etc because the tool has a built-in function for that.  We can only ever enter what the tool lets us do.  It does not support adding new featuress or new capabilities, and it has no search function.  Most of the wish-list items for Phase 2 will not be possible within the scope of the Phase 1 framework.

### A Year of Growth
ATPG just celebrated our first year anniversary on July 7, 2026, and its been a great year! Board Members have engaged in research opportunites with medical professionals, clinical trials with pharmaceuticals, drug development participation with the FDA, attending conferences with our own All Things PG booth and printed materials, created new connections and industry associations, continued our education and advocacy work with our website and PFAC activities, as well as numerous newsletters and fundraisers posted on our website. Every board member should be very proud of their achievemnts, working as a productive team!  The Phase 1 website mmight be static,  but the organization is not!

It is clear that while Phase 1 of the website remains a valuable asset, it no longer reflects the expanding scope of ATPG’s mission and long-term objectives for growth. As ATPG grows, so must the website.  More than anything, Phase 2 must allow for growth, and create the ability to add new features so that we can then add the enhancments.  The organization now has a proven track record of achievements in many areas, and the website must be able to keep pace now and into the future.

**Phase 1** was created at the beginning of our Journey, with no database and a limisted set of static HTML pages.  It represented who and what were were at the time.  That was before all the amazing growth activities of this past year, and before we received the **2026 Leo Grant** asking for enhancements to the website.  Combined, these identify a significant need for the **Phase 2** website to scale beyond a static website with no database, to a modern website that has a database.

**Therefore, the core objective of Phase 2 will be to add a Database to a website that will allow for growth.**

### Adding a Database

With a database we have the the ability for growth in any directions.  We can create a new searchable Knowledge Base (KB) dedicated to Pyoderma Gagangrenosum(PG). This KB would continuously collect content of all types, including clinical information on early diagnosis, new treatments, wound care and pain management, partner activites such as research study engagements, other rare disease associations, and Board activites engaging in Conferences, PFACs, and outreach programs like the FDA Drug Development and Pharmaceutical research and clinical trials development.  

With the ability to grow, we can expand our Clinic Finder, which can be a valuable resource to offer to patients, caregivers and other users of the website.  Right now the cllinic finder only contains 6 doctors for the whole US.  With an expanded KB we can add to new categories to the Clinic Finder, such as wound care centers, medical universities, mental health facitiles, rehabilitation centers and more, and then with a database, we can automate the process to load facilities from anywhere, even worldwide.

With the ability to grow, we can add new features to the website such as patient stories, a picture gallery, help lines, and target expanded support to those who need it most.  **PG is more than a skin disease**. It affects every area of your life, and our Phase 2 website needs to consider all those areas and how we as an organization, can help our users.

### Summary

In conclusion I'd like to offer an example of a user who might visit our website looking for help. A new caregiver just found out her husband has PG
 may ask, “How can I help my husband deal with the pain?”.  The user enters her search, just like that, and our website presents her a list of articles dealing with pain. The quality of the KB results is a function of how many articles we have entered in the database.  With a database there is no limit to how much data can be stored.  With a searchable KB we are now offering solutions, not just information, because we are solving her specific problem.

The website must also serve more than just Patients and Caregivers. Clinicians may need enhanced diagnostic and treatment options and may want to read our  patient success stories and picture gallery. Pharmaceuticals may want information about studies and clinical trials, and how to create studies for PG patients. A Patient may want to find information about the emotional impact of living with PG and seek out mental health facilities.  A database will allow us to do that.

The goal of All Things PG is to be **all** things to all those affected by Pyoderma Gangrenosum and to raise PG awareness. We need to be more than just information, which can be found by a Google or an AI search.  We want to be about solutions, for users of all types. We want to be All Things PG.
