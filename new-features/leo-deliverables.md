# Leo Deliverables for 2026 Grant

The following items have been identified as deliverables for the 2026 Leo Grant.  Most of these do not involve the website.  Click the menu item [Leo Enhancements]({{ '/new-features/leo-enhancements/' | relative_url }}) to see the short list of items specific to Phase 2. 

### 1. Website & Digital Infrastructure (All Things PG platform)

- Dedicated **All Things PG website** serving as a centralized hub for education, advocacy, research, and community-building.
- **PG Clinic/Provider Finder tool** with an interactive searchable map and provider survey/verification system.
- **Educational content library** for patients and providers (symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, multimedia tools, recorded sessions, registration links).
- **Patient & caregiver surveys** to collect real-world experience data.
- **Clinical Trials information page** listing open trials and providing education about participation.
- **Peer Support (“Buddy”) System** for research participation mentoring.
- **Secure provider-to-provider communication system**.

### 2. Patient & Community Engagement

- Establishment of a **Patient and Family Advisory Committee (PFAC)**.
- **12 PFAC meetings per year**.
- **One annual retreat** (full-day or two half-days).
- Hybrid meeting format with audiovisual support.
- Community **events, storytelling initiatives, and patient advocacy activities**.

### 3. Educational Resources & Communications

- **Standardized, expert-reviewed patient education materials**.
- **Provider education materials/toolkits**.
- **Videos and multimedia learning tools**.
- **Social Media Toolkit**.
- **Coordinated awareness campaigns**.
- **Printed educational materials** (letters/postcards).
- **Downloadable patient education resources**.

### 4. Events & Training

- **Quarterly patient-facing virtual forums**.
- **Clinician-focused training sessions**.
- Delivery of education via **Project ECHO** telementoring model.
- Case-based learning discussions.
- Ongoing CME-style provider engagement targeting **60+ clinicians**.

### 5. Research & Clinical Trial Enablement

- Dissemination of **IRB-approved research studies/surveys**.
- **Research-ready patient registry/engagement pipeline**.
- Increased **clinical trial enrollment support**.
- **Pilot research funding support** (supplies, statistical analysis, publication fees).

### 6. Marketing & Outreach

- **Digital advertising campaigns** (Google, Facebook, Instagram).
- **Email communications/newsletters**.
- **Conference presence and materials** (posters, printed collateral, sponsorship prospectus printing).

### 7. Personnel & Operational Support (to Execute Deliverables)

- Project Manager.
- Research Assistant/Coordinator.
- Patient Leaders/Committee Leads.
- Provider education facilitation team.
