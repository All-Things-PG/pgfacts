# Caregivers

Caregivers often help someone navigate appointments, treatment, wound care, daily activities, and the emotional impact of PG. Their experience is connected to the patient’s, but their questions and responsibilities are not identical.

### A caregiver-focused path

The POC gives caregivers a distinct experience choice and menu. The example navigation includes the emotional journey, treating the pain, caregiver stories, supporting a warrior, and getting help. These labels show the intended audience focus; the POC does not yet contain a complete caregiver resource collection.

### Caregiver experience goals

- Offer practical information that helps caregivers support someone with PG.
- Recognize the strain and uncertainty caregivers may experience.
- Help caregivers find relevant patient information without treating the caregiver and patient as interchangeable audiences.
- Make it easier to find support, stories, and resources for the caregiver’s own wellbeing.

Specific care instructions should be developed with appropriate clinical review. The portal’s purpose is to help people find trustworthy resources and support, not replace an individual’s care team.
