# Guest

The Guest experience is the general starting point for visitors who are exploring All Things PG or are not ready to choose a more specific path. It should introduce the site and make the main visitor experiences easy to discover.

### A welcoming general entry

The POC home page illustrates this pattern with an introduction to the community and direct choices for Patient, Caregiver, Provider, Pharmaceutical, and Guest. Guest is not a lower-access category; it is simply a broad starting point.

### Guest experience goals

- Explain what All Things PG offers and who the site serves.
- Make audience paths and major site topics easy to recognize.
- Let visitors explore without requiring registration or an account.
- Keep shared public information discoverable even when a visitor has selected another experience.

If registration or member services are added later, their purpose and requirements should be documented separately from the Guest experience.
