# Patients

The patient experience should help people living with PG find clear information and a useful next step. People may arrive soon after diagnosis, during a flare, while considering treatment, or while looking for support. The site should make room for those different moments without assuming that every patient has the same needs.

### A useful starting point

The POC patient portal groups sample content around understanding PG, treatment options, and support and community. Its patient menu also shows example paths such as the emotional journey, living with pain, patient stories, and getting help.

These are prototype examples, not a claim that the linked education, treatment guidance, or support services are complete. Final patient materials need appropriate review, clear sourcing, and careful wording about what information can and cannot do.

### Patient experience goals

- Use clear language and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Help visitors find reliable information without requiring them to know the site’s topic structure.
- Make practical resources and community support easy to discover.
- Present sensitive subjects with empathy and without blame.
- Provide clear routes to more detailed information when a visitor wants it.

The patient path should work alongside the other experiences. A patient may also be a caregiver, advocate, or researcher, and should be able to change paths or reach shared content easily.
