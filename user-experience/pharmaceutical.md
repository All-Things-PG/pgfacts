# Pharmaceutical

Pharmaceutical and industry visitors may be looking for information about PG research, unmet needs, patient perspectives, educational initiatives, or opportunities to collaborate. The experience should help them understand the organization’s public work and find relevant, clearly presented information.

### A focused public path

The POC includes Pharmaceutical as one of its visitor experiences. That demonstrates that the portal model can account for this audience alongside patients, caregivers, providers, and general visitors.

The finished content and any industry-specific functions still need to be defined. This page does not promise a private industry portal, data access, a partnership workflow, or any particular commercial arrangement.

### Experience goals

- Present All Things PG’s mission and public research-related work clearly.
- Help visitors find relevant educational, research, and collaboration information.
- Keep public information distinct from confidential, personal, or research-participant data.
- Use clear contact and review processes for future partnership inquiries.

If future work includes private collaboration or secure exchange, those capabilities should be separately scoped and designed with appropriate governance and security controls.
