# Providers

Clinicians may arrive with very different levels of familiarity with PG. A provider who rarely encounters the disease may need a concise orientation and practical references. A specialist or researcher may be looking for evidence, current research, clinical trials, or professional collaboration.

### A clinical starting point

The POC provider portal illustrates a more clinical entry page, with sample areas for clinical guidelines, research and evidence, and professional resources. Its provider menu includes example links for early diagnosis, common treatments, pain, building a care team, research, and clinical trials.

These screens show a direction for organizing provider resources. Their sample wording is not a clinical guideline or a complete, expert-reviewed provider library.

### Provider experience goals

- Make foundational PG context easy to find for clinicians who do not see the disease often.
- Provide clear routes to evidence, treatment information, research, and trials.
- Support quick scanning while making more detailed material available.
- Identify sources, review status, and update dates for clinical content.
- Keep the provider path connected to patient and caregiver perspectives where those perspectives add useful context.

The provider portal is a content and navigation experience. Any future professional communication or restricted provider service would require separate requirements, identity checks, and security design.
