# Glossary

The glossary explains terms and abbreviations used throughout the Phase 2 documentation. It will grow as the project develops.

<table class="topic-table">
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th scope="col">Term</th>
      <th scope="col">Meaning</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>All Things PG (ATPG)</strong></td>
      <td>The nonprofit organization focused on supporting people affected by pyoderma gangrenosum through information, advocacy, research, and community connections.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Clinic Finder (CF)</strong></td>
      <td>A planned searchable directory for finding relevant clinics, providers, and care resources.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Content</strong></td>
      <td>Information published for visitors, such as articles, stories, research, clinical-trial information, videos, and care resources.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Curate</strong></td>
      <td>To select, organize, review, improve, and maintain content so it is useful and appropriate for its intended audience.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>DCMS</strong></td>
      <td>Dynamic Content Management System: the proposed database-backed design for managing content, menus, routing, visitor experiences, and publication status.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Documentation site</strong></td>
      <td>This website, which presents the Phase 2 plans, analyses, designs, technical specifications, and project background for review.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Knowledge Base (KB)</strong></td>
      <td>A searchable, organized collection of reviewed information about PG and related topics for different types of visitors.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Phase 1</strong></td>
      <td>The existing All Things PG website and its initial content and capabilities.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Phase 2</strong></td>
      <td>The proposed next stage of development, adding a database, searchable content, dynamic menus, and enhanced visitor experiences.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Portal</strong></td>
      <td>A visitor-focused view of the website, tailored to the needs and interests of a particular audience.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Proof of Concept (POC)</strong></td>
      <td>A limited working demonstration used to show that a proposed technical approach or feature is feasible.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Stakeholder</strong></td>
      <td>A person or group affected by the project or involved in reviewing, shaping, funding, delivering, or using its results.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Tenant</strong></td>
      <td>An organization's isolated Microsoft 365 environment, including its domain, users, groups, applications, permissions, and collaboration services.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Tile</strong></td>
      <td>A linked panel displayed on a page to highlight related content and guide visitors toward useful next steps.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Visitor type</strong></td>
      <td>A selected audience perspective, such as Patient, Caregiver, Provider, Pharmaceutical, or Guest, used to guide the visitor experience.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
